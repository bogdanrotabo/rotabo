What it looks like

Free to use. Print it and put it up without asking us and without paying anything. We only ask that you do not change the text: the information on it is verified and has to stay true for whoever reads it.

It carries nobody's insignia. The material contains no institution's name, logo or visual identity.

Displaying it is nobody's endorsement. If a public institution chooses to display this poster, that support is strictly informative and does not constitute a recommendation, certification, accreditation, authorisation or guarantee regarding the platform.

For another size, another print run or a version in another language, write to support@rotabo.app.
