Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rotabo is operated from Switzerland and available worldwide. This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have. It applies to anyone who submits a listing, pays a fee, or visits rotabo.app.

We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who Is Responsible

The controller of your personal data is the operator of the Rotabo platform, based in Switzerland.

Contact: support@rotabo.app

2. What Data We Collect

Everyone who submits a listing (any category, seeking or offering)

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • City, country, and address
  • An optional note, or — for the Drive category, offering role — the year you obtained your driving licence and your self-declaration confirmations (licence validity, legal permission to drive, sobriety, own responsibility)
  • The category and role of your listing, and the date its visibility ends (12 months from publication)

Everything in a listing is what its author typed. Rotabo publishes it as given, without checking, approving, or correcting it, and displaying it does not make it Rotabo's statement. You are responsible for what you put in your own listing — including that it is true, that you are entitled to offer or ask for what you describe, and that publishing it is lawful where you are.

Email verification

Before a listing can be published, we send a one-time 6-digit code to the email address you provided and ask you to type it back. We temporarily store the email address, the code, and its expiry time to check this; a short-lived token confirming your email is verified is then used to let you submit your listing. This step confirms only that the address works and that whoever is submitting can read mail sent to it — it is not an identity check, and it verifies nothing else about the person or the listing.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Stripe. Rotabo does not collect, see, or store your full card number, CVC, or bank details. We receive only confirmation of a completed payment and limited metadata (amount, currency, timestamp, transaction reference) tied to your listing.

Sponsors

  • Chosen display option: business (company name, address, map location), private supporter (name, country, city), or anonymous
  • Contact email for confirmation

Viewer access (people who pay to see the listings, without publishing one)

  • Your email address, and the one-time code sent to it, to confirm the address is yours — the same verification used before a listing
  • Confirmation of a completed payment and limited metadata (amount, currency, timestamp, transaction reference) from Stripe, as above
  • The date your access runs until, stored against your email address so the site knows what you have paid for, and a random session token stored in your device's local storage so you do not have to repeat the emailed code on every visit

All visitors (browsing only, no listing submitted)

  • Basic page-visit data: which page you loaded, the referring page (if any), your browser's language setting, the country your visit came from (a two-letter country code our CDN derives from your connection — we store the code only, never your IP address), and a random identifier generated in your browser (stored in your device's local storage, not a traditional cookie) so we can distinguish separate visits
  • Aggregate traffic statistics via Google Analytics (see Section 4)
  • Standard technical data collected automatically by any web server or hosting/CDN provider (such as IP address in server logs), which we do not otherwise access or combine with the data above

4. What We Share — and With Whom

To users who have purchased viewer access, once your listing is paid and visible: your name, phone number, email, city, country, address, and note (if any) are shown to anyone holding viewer access who browses the opposite role in your category. Being found is the point of a listing — but your contact details are never public: a visitor without viewer access cannot see your phone number, email address, street address or note, and cannot open your listing.

Publicly, in the running band on the home page: while your listing is visible, the band shows your first name only — the first word of the name you entered — the category, whether you are offering it or looking for it, your town and country, and your Rotabo number if you have one. It never shows your surname, phone number, email address, street address or note. If you would rather not appear there, write to support@rotabo.app and we will remove your listing.

Sponsors: the display information you choose is published publicly on the supporters page. Anonymous sponsors are never named.

Service providers acting on our instructions:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — sending verification-code and payment-confirmation emails
  • Supabase — database hosting (EU, Frankfurt)
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery for the website itself
  • Google Analytics — aggregate, anonymised traffic statistics

Authorities: only where legally required.

We never sell your personal data.

5. After You're Connected

Rotabo does not monitor, record, or track what happens once two users contact each other. We hold no data about any arrangement, journey, exchange, or service carried out between users after they connect.

Rotabo makes the connection and nothing more. It does not vet, screen, or background-check anyone, and it holds no information that would let it tell you whether another user is trustworthy. Decide that for yourself: ask questions, verify what matters to you before agreeing to anything, and follow the law that applies to you. If a listing is false, unlawful, or harmful, tell us at support@rotabo.app and we will look at it.

6. International Transfers

Rotabo operates from Switzerland and serves users worldwide. Our database is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt); some of the service providers listed in Section 4, such as GitHub and Google, are based outside Switzerland and the EEA. Where data is transferred outside Switzerland or the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Published listings: visible for 12 months from publication, then hidden; the underlying record may be kept for a limited additional period for support and accounting purposes
  • Listings whose email was never verified, and listings from the earlier period that were never paid for: kept for a limited period, then deleted
  • Email verification codes: deleted or expire automatically shortly after use, and in any case within about 10 minutes of being sent
  • Payment and accounting records: as required by Swiss law (generally up to 10 years)
  • Sponsor display data: until you ask us to remove it
  • Site-visit analytics: kept in aggregate for measuring traffic; not linked to your listing data

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you
  • correct inaccurate data
  • request deletion of your data (for example, removing a listing early)
  • object to or restrict certain processing
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example, sponsor display)
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Switzerland, the FDPIC; in the EU, your national authority)

To exercise any right, write to support@rotabo.app.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), restricted access to data (an internal, password-protected view of listings and traffic used only to operate the platform), and rely on Stripe's certified infrastructure for all payment handling. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

We don't use traditional tracking cookies for our own features. We use your browser's local storage for things strictly necessary for the platform to work: your language preference, a random per-browser identifier used for page-visit analytics, and — after you verify a code — a short-lived marker so you don't have to re-verify the same email again within the same browsing session. Google Analytics sets its own cookies for aggregate traffic statistics; you can opt out using your browser's or Google's own tools at any time.

11. Age Requirement

Rotabo is not intended for anyone under 18 to submit a listing. We do not knowingly collect listing data from minors. Some categories (see the Terms of Service) may involve arrangements where children or other people of any age are present or benefit from what's arranged, even though the Rotabo user submitting the listing must be an adult. If you believe a minor has submitted a listing, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy, including as categories, features, or service providers change. The current version is always available on the platform, with the date of last update shown above.

Contact for any privacy question: support@rotabo.app
