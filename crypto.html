What this is

Said plainly, because the plain version is the one worth reading.

A community token

It is launched on pump.fun, a public launchpad on Solana. Anyone can buy it and anyone can sell it, at whatever price the market makes.

It carries the Rotabo name

The name and the mark come from rotabo.app, the marketplace where people post what they need and what they offer.

It is not a share in the company

Holding it gives no ownership, no dividend, no vote and no claim on Rotabo or on anything Rotabo earns.

How to buy it

Four steps. The whole thing takes about ten minutes the first time and under a minute after that.

1

Get a Solana wallet

Phantom or Solflare, as a phone app or a browser extension. Write the recovery phrase down on paper and never type it anywhere else.

2

Put some SOL in it

Buy SOL on any exchange and send it to your wallet address, or buy it inside the wallet itself. Keep a little spare for network fees.

3

Open the official link

Use the button above, or paste the contract address from this page into pump.fun. Check it character by character.

4

Swap

Enter how much SOL you want to spend and confirm in your wallet. That is it.

Before you buy anything

This is not an investment, and not advice

A token launched on pump.fun has no guaranteed value. Its price is whatever someone else will pay for it at that moment, it can fall to nothing, and most tokens launched this way do. Nobody at Rotabo can promise you a price, a return, or a buyer.

Spend only what you would be relaxed about losing entirely. Not rent, not savings, not borrowed money.

Nothing on this page is financial advice. If you are not sure what any of it means, that is a good reason not to buy.

There will be fakes

Every launch attracts copies with the same name, the same picture and a different address. The only address we will ever publish is the one on this page, at rotabo.app/crypto.html.

Nobody from Rotabo will ever message you first, ask for your recovery phrase, ask you to connect your wallet to a site to "verify" it, or send you a private link. Any of those is somebody stealing from you.
