The Bridge — a translator when you need one, for life as an immigrant

You're far from home, in a country whose language you barely grasp, and every official letter tightens your stomach. At the counter, at the doctor's, at your child's school, or on the phone with some agency — you don't need a translator all the time, just exactly when it matters. I come with you wherever you need me and translate on the spot, so you understand what's happening and what you have to do. I break official letters down into plain words: what it is, by when, where to sign. I make the calls for you and speak the local language, while you sit beside me at ease. We fill out the applications together, step by step, at the right office. I don't just translate the language — I translate peace of mind, and I hold your hand until you get where you need to be. From “I don't understand” to “I've got this.”
