## 1. What Rotabo Is — and What It Is Not

Rotabo is a **technology platform** operated from Switzerland that connects people who should not drive (because of alcohol or other impairing substances) with independent drivers willing to drive the client's own car.

Rotabo is **not**:
- a transport company, taxi service, or ride-hailing operator;
- an employer, agent, or representative of any driver;
- a party to any agreement between a client and a driver;
- a guarantor that any ride will take place, be completed, or be carried out in any particular way.

Rotabo's role ends at the moment of connection. Everything that happens after two users are connected — pickup, route, fare, payment, and the ride itself — is arranged and carried out **exclusively between the client and the driver**, at their own responsibility.

## 2. The Cause

Rotabo exists to support one cause: **no one should drive under the influence of alcohol or other impairing substances when a safer way home exists.** All contributions made through the platform are voluntary donations in support of this cause.

## 3. Definitions

## 5. How the Platform Works

**For clients (A):**
1. A client submits a ride request and makes a contribution to activate the search.
2. The request becomes visible to available drivers.
3. When a driver accepts (by making their own contribution), the two are connected and exchange contact details.
4. The remaining fare (approximately 90% of the ride value) is agreed and settled **directly between client and driver**, in cash or by card, outside the platform.

**For drivers (B):**
1. Browsing open requests is free.
2. To accept a request, a driver makes a contribution of 10% of the ride value. The first driver to contribute secures the ride exclusively.
3. After connection, the driver arranges pickup and all ride details directly with the client.

**After connection:** Rotabo does not monitor, verify, or supervise whether or how the ride takes place. The platform's involvement ends at connection.
